Craft

From sketch to hallmark

  1. Design

    Style, size and weight are agreed with you before any gold is melted.

  2. Casting

    Gold is alloyed to the karat you chose, cast, then cleaned and shaped.

  3. Setting and finish

    Stones are set by hand. The piece is polished, or given a matte or antique finish.

  4. Hallmarking

    Purity is tested and the piece is marked at a BIS-recognised centre with its HUID.

Purity

Know what you are buying

Karat tells you how much of the piece is gold. Hallmark numbers show the same thing in parts per thousand.
